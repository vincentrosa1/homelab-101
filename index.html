Run your first home server

A homelab is a computer at home that runs services for you, like a media server for your movies, a game server for your friends, or a backup spot for your photos. It can be an old laptop you already own.

This guide takes you from picking hardware to running your first project, with the safety basics along the way. You don't need Linux or networking experience to follow along.
